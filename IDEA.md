# InsureOne — Product Idea

## What it is
A mobile app by **Roinet Insurance Brokers** that becomes the customer's lifelong insurance home — not just a place to buy policies, but a place to live with them.

## The Problem
After you buy insurance, the company forgets you.
- You juggle 4–6 policies across different apps and emails
- Filing a claim takes 20+ minutes with no real-time updates
- No advisor, no family planning, no proactive help

## The Solution — 4 Pillars

### 1. One Wallet for All Policies
All policies (health, car, life, travel) from any insurer, in one place. Auto-imported via Bima Sugam (IRDAI's new "UPI for Insurance" rail).

### 2. 60-Second AI Claims
- Photo/video → AI fills the form → live tracker
- Cashless settlement at network hospitals/garages
- If insurer delays or rejects, app auto-escalates (even files with IRDAI Ombudsman)

### 3. Money + Life Hub
Not just insurance — daily-use features that bring users back weekly:
- Mutual funds, stocks, SIP tracking
- Loans marketplace (home, car, personal, education)
- Credit cards portal
- FASTag, RTO challan, PUC, doctor-on-call, cashless hospital finder

### 4. Personal Relationship
- Named human advisor (concierge) for top-tier customers
- Family Protection Plan (PSIP) — life events, coverage gaps
- **Soft Benefits Wallet** = 10% of annual premium back as perks (movies, dinners, hotels)
- Smart renewal nudges 30 days before policy expiry

## Who It's For
- **Free Tier** — Anyone. Upload any policy PDF, get an AI summary, hospital finder, FAQs.
- **Roinet Member** — Full experience: wallet, advisor, family plan, soft benefits.
